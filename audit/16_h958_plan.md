## The leaderboard's fourth decimal

Kaggle shows three decimals. Inside a block of equal displayed scores, the **rank follows the unrounded score**. A gain that leaves the displayed score unchanged therefore still moves the team up inside its block.

The absolute rank is not a usable signal: it moved 182 → 185 in about 70 min on 10-14 with no change on our side. The usable signal is **GetLeaderboard's team-best submissionId**. A new submission that shows 0.957 has beaten the team best only if that id switches to it.

# Part 2, optional: ensemble-teacher distillation (H959b)

## Why

Our largest single gain came from the **teacher**. Changing reader targets from report labels plus our own out-of-fold predictions (B, 0.939) to report labels plus the Mirror teacher (C, 0.948) gave +0.009 on one model. The next step is a 0.956-level teacher: the public blend itself, run on the train studies.

## The teacher T2 (form fixed 2026-10-15, before any teacher ran)

T2 is a per-finding **rank blend** with the 0.956 blend's own weights:
- Mirror384 0.325, M224 (+mirror) 0.175, R7 0.5 (from Raptor 0.5 = 0.65 × 384 + 0.35 × 224, and R7 0.5);
- each member is ranked over the studies it scored, and the weights are renormalised per study;
- the result is mapped onto **Mirror384's per-finding marginal**.

Why this form:
- **One variable.** The student targets keep exactly C3's scale (0.25 v0 + 0.75 teacher), so E75 differs from C3 only in the teacher's ordering.
- **Faithful weights.** A probability average would give R7 an effective share of 0.31–0.58 instead of 0.5, because the members are calibrated differently. Mirror averages about 0.45 against about 0.29 for v0, and R7 uses an lr-prior.

**The CPU gate, `ours_leg/gpjw/gate_ens_teacher.py`.** It runs locally on the downloaded teacher outputs, before any GPU is spent on students. It writes `ens_train_raw.csv` and `ens_gate_report.json`.

Stop rules (fixed now):
- a member covers 90% of the train studies or less, or the R7 shards are not 0..N−1;
- a member's column is constant (SD < 1e-3);
- a member's per-finding Spearman vs Mirror384 is below 0.2 (a swapped or misaligned column);
- a member's macro AUC vs v0 is below 0.85 (Mirror alone: 0.9255).

Everything else in the report is diagnostic: effective shares, Spearman of the probability average vs the rank blend, and gold-58 AUC. Tests on synthetic teachers pass: the PASS case and all four stop cases. The student kernel reads the gated file with exactly C3's code path; only the file name differs.
