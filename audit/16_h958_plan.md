## The leaderboard's fourth decimal

Kaggle shows three decimals. Inside a block of equal displayed scores, the **rank follows the unrounded score**. So a gain that leaves the displayed score unchanged still moves a team up inside its block.

On 2026-10-14 we were at rank 182 inside the 0.957 block. A better notebook should move that rank up even if it still shows 0.957, and it may cross to 0.958.

# Final experiment toward 0.958–0.960: ensemble-teacher distillation (added 2026-10-15)

## Why

Our largest single gain came from the **teacher**. Changing reader targets from report labels plus our own out-of-fold predictions (B, 0.939) to report labels plus the Mirror teacher (C, 0.948) gave +0.009 on one model. The Mirror teacher is a 0.949-level model.

Forum signals point the same way:
- azamat1ch (LB #7): "labels… AI, not by hand, don't rely on reports alone"
- datnt114: 0.961 with own models

The next step is a **0.956-level teacher**: the public blend itself, run on the train studies.

## The teacher

T2 = 0.325 · Mirror384 + 0.175 · M224(+mirror) + 0.5 · R7, in probabilities. These are exactly the 0.956 blend's weights:
- Raptor 0.5 = 0.65·384 + 0.35·224
- R7 0.5

When a member did not score a study, the weights are renormalised for that study. A member covering less than 90% of the train studies stops the kernel.

**Students:** the C3 recipe (targets = 0.25·v0 + 0.75·T2; the best of our recipes, leg 0.949), with two backbones: E75t (convnext_tiny) and E75s (convnext_small).
