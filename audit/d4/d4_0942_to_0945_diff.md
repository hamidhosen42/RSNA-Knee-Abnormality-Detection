## 2. Exact code change set 0.943 → D4 Lite

1. One token, line 3003 of the diff: `_RAD_EXCLUDE = ("Baker's", 'Fracture')` → `("Baker's",)`.
   - This idea was already public at 0.942: `sushanthtiruvaipati/rsna-knee-0942-rad-fracture-oof`,
     one token changed from maverick's 0.942, scored 0.942 (no visible change).
   - Its author reports (unverified by us) a report-label OOF gain for Fracture of +0.0118
     (n=4185, 156 pos, 4 of 5 folds positive) and a macro gain of +0.00099, **but −0.0097 on the
     58 gold studies** (18 Fracture positives).
2. Cells 30–32 appended: the private fleet leg at 0.45 rank weight.

Nothing else differs, including preprocessing, checkpoints, TTA and every other weight.

## 3. Where the +0.002 comes from — bounds, not attribution

Public LB is shown to 3 decimals, so each score is an interval:

| Pair | Displayed Δ | Δ possible from rounding alone |
|---|---|---|
| 0.943 − OLD-0.942 | +0.001 | (0.000, 0.002) |
| LITE − 0.943 | +0.002 | (0.001, 0.003) |
| BLEND − LITE | +0.001 | (0.000, 0.002) |
| LITE − OLD-0.942 | +0.003 | (0.002, 0.004) |

LB sampling noise comes on top of this (the public split is a subset of an unknown-size hidden
test; the D4 author says "~1300" studies, unverified). Paired differences between highly correlated
submissions are much less noisy than the scores themselves, but I cannot quantify that without
predictions.

What the evidence supports:
- **Fracture flip:** small effect expected.
  - In the maverick family the identical flip moved 0.942 → 0.942.
  - In D4 it only touches 1/12 of the macro, and only the 25% transformer share of the Fracture
    column before the 0.55 public weight. The Rad share in the public part's Fracture column goes
    0.05 → 0.16.
  - Report-label and gold-58 evidence point in opposite directions.
- **Private leg:** by elimination, most of the +0.001 to +0.003 is *plausibly* from the 45%
  EfficientNetV2-S leg. This is **not established**: no ablation has been run, and the leg's
  weights, training data and OOF are inaccessible.

Answer to "why does D4 Lite score 0.945?":
- **Established:** it is the public 0.943 pipeline, plus a known 0.942-era Fracture routing flip,
  plus a 45% rank blend with a private EfficientNetV2-S model that is architecturally independent of
  the public stack.
- **Plausible, unproven:** the independent CNN leg is the source of the gain.
