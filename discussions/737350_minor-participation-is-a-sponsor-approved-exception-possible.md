We welcome participation of competitors between 13-18 years of age after the following forms are submitted.

Form #1: Parent/Guardian Consent for Minor's Use of Kaggle.com Submitted one time by a parent or guardian to authorize a minor child (between ages 13 and 16) to create and use a Kaggle account. https://www.kaggle.com/guardian-consent-minor-use Form #2: Parent/Guardian Consent for Minor's Participation in a Kaggle Competition Submitted on a per-competition basis for the minor to enter and participate in a specific, eligible Kaggle competition. https://www.kaggle.com/consent-minors-process

Thank you and good luck!
