When you want to get into teams it's always a good idea to have your bio updated.  Right now yours is a blank slate.  

One issue that folks have with "team mates" is you often end up getting ghosted by someone who thought kaggle was fun but decided later not so much fun.  Team mates can get you disqualified, so signing up with random strangers is risky.  Put the team wanted icon - so change the setting to show your looking for team maters.  

With your blank profile the odds of a top team wanting you are pretty slim.

Here are some reasons I would join up with a person.

1.  They come from the same country / University / Football team, etc.
2.  The have LinkedIn / Github etc showing where I can go look at more about you.
3.  They have lots of current year kaggle activity - this is a big one for me, I need to know that your really into kaggle and have worked hard on one or more competitions.
4.  They have placed in a decent number of competitions in the metal.  
5.  They have a work schedule close to mine.  Hard to team up if my working hours and yours never overlap.
6.  You have worked on competitions as a team member.  Small teams can really gel or it can be three months of havoc.  If you have worked on multiple teams I understand that you at least understand team dynamics.

Guess I did not answer your specific question - how to email.

A team active in looking for members - they will have the icon showing on the Leader board.  When you click on it there is a box to contact the lead.

If not looking for members - on the leader board click on the persons icon.  It should take you to profile which has a contact button.

You have a budget of contacts you can make per month - I think the number is still based on your kaggle experience level.  It might not be a very large number for a fresh face like yourself.  I have been here 9 years and allowed only 20 emails per month.

I did not mention this before but one other issue - your message needs to have spelling correct, punctuation, some paragraphs, ie, it needs to look professional.  I think your getting all those down votes because your message is a bit of a mess.
