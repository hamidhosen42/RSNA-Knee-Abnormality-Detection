Forgive me if this is already known by most. I am new to Kaggle competitions but have really jumped in the last few months.

My read on the current status of this competition is that many of the top public notebooks were built, at least in part, with OAI training data, which is most likely a violation of the prize eligibility rules.

I wasn't initially concerned about this, but it's now clear that many hundreds of people have copied those notebooks and submitted them. It seems likely that most of the people who will finish in medal position will do it on the backs of these notebooks. 

Does "prize eligibility" include medals? Is there a review process that will strip medals from those who violate the rules? It's not hard for Kaggle to see who used what and where it came from.

I and many other work very hard on these competitions, both as learning tools and portfolio projects. How do I stick this in my portfolio and say "I finished in the top X% but most of the people above me didn't follow the rules"? That's not a good look for anyone.
